# AxtarIS Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

These Community Guidelines are part of the AxtarIS **Terms of Service**. They exist so that AxtarIS stays what it is meant to be: a safe, honest place to find work and to hire. They apply to everything you publish or send on the Service — profiles, CVs, vacancies, applications, messages, and images.

## 1. The short version

Be honest. Be respectful. Never ask candidates for money. Report what looks wrong.

## 2. Not allowed anywhere on AxtarIS

- **Illegal content or activity** of any kind.
- **Fraud and scams** — including advance-fee recruitment scams ("pay for processing/visa/equipment"), phishing, and attempts to move people to untraceable payment channels.
- **Fake identity** — impersonating a person or company, fake vacancies, fabricated credentials.
- **Harassment and hate** — threats, insults, unwanted sexual attention, or attacks on people for who they are (ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, or similar).
- **Sexual, violent, or shocking content** — the Service is a professional space.
- **Spam and off-purpose use** — bulk unsolicited messages, advertising unrelated to recruiting, scraping or harvesting user data.
- **Malware and security abuse** — links to malicious software, attempts to break or probe the Service.

## 3. Rules for vacancies and employers

A vacancy on AxtarIS must be a **real, currently open job** with the real employer identified. In addition:

- **Never charge candidates.** No fees, deposits, or purchases at any stage — application, interview, training, equipment, "registration". Asking candidates for money is the fastest way to lose your account permanently.
- **No discriminatory requirements** that are unrelated to the genuine needs of the job (gender, age, religion, ethnicity, disability, family status, and similar).
- **No pyramid or MLM schemes**, and no "opportunities" disguised as employment.
- **Describe the job honestly** — duties, pay (if stated), hours, and location must not be materially misleading.
- **Use candidate data only for hiring** for the role concerned — see the Employer Terms.

## 4. Rules for chat

Chat exists to talk about work — applications, interviews, job details.

- No harassment, pressure, or discrimination.
- No requests for money, card details, or documents beyond what a legitimate hiring process needs.
- No moving people off-platform to avoid these rules.
- Employers: keep it professional; candidates are evaluating you too.

## 5. Report and block

- **Report** any vacancy, profile, or message that breaks these rules — use the report button in the app (on profiles, vacancies, and in chat).
- **Block** any user in chat to immediately stop them contacting you.
- Reports are confidential; the reported user does not see who reported them.

**We review reports within 24 hours** and act faster on urgent safety issues.

## 6. Enforcement

We apply measures proportionate to the violation:

1. **Warning** and/or removal of the offending content;
2. **Feature restriction** (e.g. posting or messaging suspended);
3. **Account suspension**;
4. **Permanent ban** — immediate for serious cases: scams, charging candidates, threats, fake identity.

Where practicable we tell you what was removed and why.

## 7. Appeals

If you believe we got it wrong, write to **info@axtaris.app** within 30 days with the details. A person — not an automated system — will review the decision and reply.
